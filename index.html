Purpose of This Site

This site is my personal cybersecurity learning archive and public writeup library. I write notes from TryHackMe boxes, Hack The Box machines, CTF practice, cybersecurity resources, and my own lab work.

My goal is to help the community, document my learning journey, and build a revision library that I can use later for CRTA preparation, red team practice, Active Directory labs, and privilege escalation review.

Writeup Rules

I publish learning-focused writeups only. I do not publish flags, direct answers, private-room solutions, copied premium content, machine-specific passwords, or anything that ruins the learning experience for others.

My notes focus on concepts, methodology, commands, mistakes, lessons learned, and safe explanations that can help beginners understand cybersecurity properly.
